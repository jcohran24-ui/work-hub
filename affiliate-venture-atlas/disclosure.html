Affiliate disclosure & how we choose tools

Venture Atlas publishes educational articles and comparisons about online-business software. Some links may eventually be affiliate links, meaning we may receive a commission when a reader buys or subscribes through the link, at no extra cost to the reader.

Current launch status

As of the initial launch, the site uses ordinary links to software providers' official websites. No affiliate tracking IDs have been configured, and clicking these links does not earn Venture Atlas an affiliate commission.

How editorial recommendations work

We explain the types of businesses each tool may serve, based on publicly described capabilities. We do not claim we personally tested software that we have not tested. We do not sell favorable reviews or invent ratings, endorsements, user counts, or income results.

When affiliate relationships become active, a plain-language notice will appear near the relevant links. Affiliate payments should not override basic suitability, user needs, price transparency or product limitations. Readers should verify pricing, plan availability, taxes, and provider terms independently.

Questions or corrections

We intend to keep product descriptions current, but software changes quickly. If you identify an error, please check the provider's official documentation while we update the page. Contact methods will be added when a dedicated business email address is configured.

For disclosure guidance, see the FTC Endorsement Guides.
