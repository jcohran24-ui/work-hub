Affiliate disclosure & how we choose tools

Idea Trellis publishes educational articles and comparisons about online-business software. Some links are affiliate links, meaning we may receive a commission when a reader makes a qualifying purchase or subscription through the link, at no additional cost to the reader.

Current launch status

Our systeme.io links contain a referral tracking URL that may credit Idea Trellis for qualifying purchases. Shopify, Kit and other vendors currently use ordinary official-site links unless we specifically mark them as affiliate links. Commissions and attribution are subject to each program's rules.

How editorial recommendations work

We explain the types of businesses each tool may serve, based on publicly described capabilities. We do not claim we personally tested software that we have not tested. We do not sell favorable reviews or invent ratings, endorsements, user counts, or income results.

We display a plain-language affiliate disclosure near referral links. Affiliate payments should not override basic suitability, user needs, price transparency or product limitations. Readers should verify pricing, plan availability, taxes, and provider terms independently.

Questions or corrections

We intend to keep product descriptions current, but software changes quickly. If you identify an error, please check the provider's official documentation while we update the page. Contact methods will be added when a dedicated business email address is configured.
